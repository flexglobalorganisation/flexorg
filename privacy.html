Who we are

This website, connect.flexglobal.org, is run by the Foundation for Leadership Excellence (FLEX), Ghana. It shares information about the FLEXConnect Series, a monthly leadership conversation. You can contact us about privacy at [your email address].

What we collect

  • Just browsing. You can read this site without giving us any personal details. Our website host (GitHub Pages) may keep ordinary technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the service.
  • Analytics (only with your permission, and only if switched on). Anonymous information about visits, such as pages viewed and the type of device.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and, where they apply to you, the GDPR and UK GDPR, you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email us at [your email address] and we will respond promptly. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ghana, or to your local data protection authority.

How long we keep things

Analytics data, if switched on, is kept in anonymous form for as long as is useful for improving the site.

Children

This site is aimed at adults and young professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us their details, please tell us and we will remove them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use cookies or personal data, we will update this page and the date above.
